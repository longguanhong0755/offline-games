    How to play

    Guess the word in 6 tries!

    After each guess, the tiles show whether your letters occur earlier or later in the alphabet compared to the correct letters. Earlier means closer to A, later means closer to Z.

    p

    Letter is correct

    d

    Letter is too early in the alphabet

    w

    Letter is too late in the alphabet

    e ...v

    The range of possible letters is shown on the tile and the keyboard as you type
