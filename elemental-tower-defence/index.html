📜 GAMEPLAY INSTRUCTIONS & RULES

1

🏰 Building & Tower Lifespan (15 Seconds)

Select any element card from the Element Shop on the right sidebar and click on any empty green tile on the map to place a tower. Every tower has a 15-second lifespan timer (`15s`, `10s`, `3s`).

💡 Pro Tip: Upgrading a tower fully repairs its Health (HP) and resets its lifespan timer back to 15 Seconds 100% Full!

2

🎯 Tower Targeting & Upgrades

Click on any active placed tower to open its Tower Detail Panel. You can customize target priority:

  • First: Shoots the creep furthest along the path.
  • Last: Shoots the creep trailing behind.
  • Strong: Targets high-HP Armored Tanks & Bosses.
  • Weak: Finishes off low-HP creeps quickly.
3

👾 Enemy Counter-Attacks & Tower Health

Beware! Heavy enemies like Armored Juggernauts, Flying Sky Vipers, and OMEGA Titan Bosses will shoot EMP lasers and drop plasma bombs back at your towers to damage and destroy them! Keep an eye on your towers' HP bars under their base.

4

💥 Elemental Combo Synergies

Combine multiple elemental types on the same enemy creep to trigger devastating combo explosions:

  • Fire 🔥 + Ice ❄️: Steam Blast (+150% Instant Area AOE Damage)
  • Lightning ⚡ + Ice ❄️: Superconduct (-50% Enemy Armor Reduction)
  • Fire 🔥 + Lightning ⚡: Plasma Shockwave (Chain Lightning AOE)
5

☄️ Auto Ultimate Meteor Strike & Restock Cooldowns

Every creep kill charges your Ultimate Meter in the header dashboard. Once it reaches 100%, an Auto Meteor Strike will rain down and obliterate all creeps on screen!

Powerful towers like the Tesla Spire ⚡ have a 20-second shop restock cooldown badge (`🔒 20s`).
