Roguelike Idle

Descendite et videte._

✦·✦

Roguelike Idle is an unfinished hybrid: classic top-down 2D roguelike dungeons (generated with Wave Function Collapse) that your character traverses without your direct input.

The interesting design question is: how do you make an autoplay roguelike still interesting? The answer was supposed to be tactics as equippables/loot - but only bare functionality is implemented as yet. Rendered with PixiJS.
