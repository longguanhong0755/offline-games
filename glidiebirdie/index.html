On desktop: Space or click to flap, Shift to soften descent, Down Arrow to dive, Escape to pause, M to mute, R to restart, F for fullscreen, P for postcard, C to save a picture. On phone: use the on-screen Brake, Flap, and Dive buttons below the canvas. With a gamepad: Cross or A flaps, left shoulder or left trigger brakes, right shoulder, right trigger, or D-pad down dives, Start pauses, Back mutes.

Comfort-first design notes

Balanced typography, reduced-motion awareness, better spacing, higher legibility, and normalized simulation timing keep the experience calm across laptops, desktops, phones, and high-refresh-rate displays.

Tip: the bird is forgiving. Tap lightly, hold Brake on a phone (or Shift on a keyboard) to soften descents, and play for flow.
