Shortcut keys

F1 - show/hide this help
PgUp,PgDn - move camera up/down (default is 1.7m)
W,A,S,D - walking, space jumps, hold shift to walk slow
M - toggle minimap on/off
N - choose different map
E - enable/disable editor mode:
H - change mode to adding houses (click on ground to add house)
R - change mode to adding roads (click on ground to add road)
O - change orientation of the house you are pointing at (ccw by 90°)
V - when pointing on side of the house, cycle through ads
F - when pointing on the house, toggle fire escape on/off
G - when pointing on the house, cycle through signage
L - when pointing on the ground, add/remove lamp
B - toggle house alwaysVisible (for houses at the end of long road)
Ctrl+Z - undo last change
F2 - save world (houses, roads, lamps,...)
F3 - export world to *.nws file, F4 import *.nws file
1,2,3,4,5 - change number of floors of selected house
