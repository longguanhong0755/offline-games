HOW TO PLAY

You never walk. You move only by dashing toward the cursor — as often as you want. Survive, chain hits, and break your record.

DASHES

  • E Phantom Dash cyan afterimages
  • W Blink Dash you vanish mid-dash
  • A Phase Dash longest i-frames
  • B Reversal Dash recoil, then launch
  • R Rupture Dash your attack

RULES

  • 3 lives. A hit costs one and clears nearby bullets.
  • Dashes make you briefly untouchable — but spamming drains PHASE and the protection fades until it refills.
  • Pillars block bullets. Attacks commit you for a moment.
  • Hits build your chain and multiplier; getting hit resets it.
  • Graze bullets without i-frames for bonus points.

COMBOS press together

  • E+W Cross Break
  • E+A Phantom Pierce
  • E+B Shockwave
  • W+A Spiral Cut
  • W+B Afterimage
  • A+B Rift Split

  • E+A+B Breaker Wave erases bullets
  • W+A+B Rift Collapse
  • E+W+A+B ZERO//BREAK
