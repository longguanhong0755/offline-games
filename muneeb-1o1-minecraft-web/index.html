How To Play

Movement: Use WASD to walk, Space to jump, Shift to sneak.

Mining & Building: Hold Left Click to mine blocks. Right Click to place blocks from your hotbar.

Inventory & Crafting: Press E to open inventory. Place 4 wood logs to make planks, then craft a Crafting Table to unlock the 3x3 crafting grid!

Creative Mode: Press F or double-tap Space to fly. You have infinite blocks and break things instantly.

Sounds: All audio is generated in real-time with Web Audio API synthesizer. Click anywhere to activate audio!
